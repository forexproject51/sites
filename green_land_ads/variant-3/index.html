We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK visitors)

Where applicable, we process data based on: your consent (analytics cookies), our legitimate interest in operating and improving the site, and compliance with legal obligations.

7. Data retention

Cookie consent records are stored for up to 6 months. Analytics data, if enabled, is retained according to the analytics provider's settings. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us through the community channel linked from this page.
