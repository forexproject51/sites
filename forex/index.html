Forex Explained

What is “Forex” trading?

Forex, or foreign exchange trading, is the global market for currency trading. With a daily volume over $5 trillion, it is the largest and most liquid market worldwide, operating 24 hours a day, five days a week. To succeed in Forex, one must understand the market, remain disciplined, and have a well-planned investment strategy.
